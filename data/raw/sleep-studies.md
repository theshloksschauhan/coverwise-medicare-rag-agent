# Sleep studies
Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers Type I, II, III, and IV sleep tests and devices if you’re eligible
- Covered by Part B
- After the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount
- Who's eligible
- Facility
Sleep studies monitor your sleep patterns, breathing, and movement to find out if you have a sleep disorder.
## Who's eligible
You have clinical signs and symptoms of sleep apnea.
After you meet the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount .
## Facility
Medicare only covers Type I tests if you get them in a sleep lab facility. Your doctor or other health care provider must order the sleep test.
