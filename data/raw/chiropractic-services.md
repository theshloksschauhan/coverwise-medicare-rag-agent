# Chiropractic services
Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) only covers certain chiropractic services.
- Covered by Part B
- After the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount
Chiropractic services include techniques (like adjustments to the spine or other parts of the body) to relieve body pain.
Part B only covers adjustments of the spine by a chiropractor to correct a subluxation (when the spinal joints fail to move properly, but the contact between the joints remains intact) when your chiropractor uses their hands or a device called an "activator".
Medicare doesn't cover other services or tests a chiropractor orders, including X-rays, massage therapy, and acupuncture .
After the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount .
