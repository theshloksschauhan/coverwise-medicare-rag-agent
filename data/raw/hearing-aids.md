# Hearing aids
Medicare doesn’t cover hearing aids or exams for fitting hearing aids
- Not Covered
- You pay all costs for non-covered services, including hearing aids and exams.
Hearing aids are electronic devices that help people with hearing loss hear better.
Some Medicare Advantage Plans (Part C) offer extra benefits that Original Medicare doesn’t cover - like vision, hearing, or dental. Contact the plan for more information.
You pay all costs.
