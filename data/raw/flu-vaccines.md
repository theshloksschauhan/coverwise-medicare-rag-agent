# Flu vaccines
Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers the seasonal flu vaccine
- Covered by Part B
- Preventive Service
- Usually once each flu season
- You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment
- How often
- Facility
Flu vaccines can keep you from getting sick with seasonal influenza (flu) viruses during the fall and winter.
This 2026-2027 flu season you can get a trivalent flu vaccine that protects against 3 different flu viruses. People 65 and older can get a trivalent flu vaccine to lower the risk of hospital visits and death. The trivalent vaccine is safe and effective. Get more details about the vaccine.
How can I protect myself from other respiratory viruses?
You pay nothing for a flu vaccine if your doctor or other health care provider accepts assignment for giving the vaccine.
## Facility
You can get your flu vaccine at many places, including your doctor’s office and your local pharmacy — contact them for details. Find Flu Vaccines.
## How often
Usually one vaccine each flu season.
