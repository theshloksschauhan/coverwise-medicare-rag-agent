# X-rays
Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers medically necessary diagnostic X-rays
- Covered by Part B
Your treating doctor or other health care provider must order the X-rays
- After you meet the Part B deductible , you pay 20% of the Medicare-approved amount .
- In a hospital outpatient setting , you also pay a separate facility copayment .
