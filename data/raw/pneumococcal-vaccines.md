# Pneumococcal vaccines
Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers pneumococcal vaccines
- Covered by Part B
- Preventive Service
You pay nothing if your provider accepts assignment
## Description
Pneumococcal vaccines protect against different strains of the bacteria that cause pneumonia.
## Coverage details
Talk with your doctor or other health care provider about which vaccines are right for you.
## Costs
You pay nothing if your doctor or health care provider accepts assignment for giving the vaccine.
